What's new on the Property Sauce system

A quick update for the office — 25 September 2026

New: a phone number that answers itself

Property Sauce now has its own UK mobile number, +44 7457 410735. It answers calls automatically and can send one-time sign-in codes by text. Tenants and landlords also now have the option to sign in to the website with just their mobile number, not only email.

New: the to-let listings page

propertysauce.co/to-let/ now shows every property currently available to rent, straight from Zoho. This is fully automatic:

Set a property's Status to "To Let" on its Landlord record in Zoho, with a rent figure filled in, and it appears on the website within minutes. No other step needed. Take it off To Let, or clear the rent, and it comes down the same way.

Someone clicking "Register interest" on a listing opens our website chat, which passes their enquiry straight to the team.

Fixed: two real issues

  • Signing in to the tenant/landlord portal by mobile number was silently broken for everyone since it was added — now fixed.
  • A tenant's document list was showing everything on the property file, including the landlord's mortgage statements and insurance certificate. Now a tenant only ever sees their tenancy-related documents (gas, electrical, EPC, licence, inventory, deposit, tenancy agreement) — nothing about the landlord's finances.

Coming next

Automated pre-qualification and viewing booking from the website chat, automatic OpenRent posting when a property goes To Let, and a proper phone system that can act on a call, not just take a message.
