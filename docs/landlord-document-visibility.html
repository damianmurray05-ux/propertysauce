Landlord portal — what a landlord sees

Companion to the tenant document list — 25 September 2026

Unlike the tenant side, the landlord portal is unrestricted by design: a signed-in landlord sees every document on their own property, because it is their own file. Nothing is filtered out. This page lists what that means in practice, for approval alongside the tenant list.

One landlord's portal never shows another landlord's property — that boundary is by ownership (the "Established Landlord" field on the property), not by document type. Within their own property, a landlord sees everything.

How it's scoped

The same code that restricts a tenant's list (src/chat/tenantfile.mjs) is never applied to the landlord portal (src/chat/landlords.mjs) — they are two separate code paths on purpose, so a change meant to protect tenant privacy can never accidentally start hiding a landlord's own documents from them.
