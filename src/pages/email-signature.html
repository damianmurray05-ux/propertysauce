Email signature and footer.

One signature for every Property Sauce mailbox, signed by the team rather than a person. Pick the mailbox, press copy, paste into Gmail. The legal footer is built in, and there is a separate copy of it for Google Workspace's append-footer setting.

Copied. Paste it into Gmail.

Put it in Gmail.

Two minutes per mailbox.

  1. Sign in to Gmail as the mailbox. Click the gear, then See all settings, then the General tab.
  2. Scroll to Signature and click Create new. Name it Property Sauce.
  3. Click in the box and paste.
  4. Below the box, set For new emails use and On reply/forward use to Property Sauce.
  5. Scroll to the bottom and click Save changes. Send yourself a test.

Phones: the Gmail app uses its own mobile signature. In the app, Settings, the account, Mobile signature, paste the plain-text version.
