Service charges and accounts

An annual budget built from the lease and the building, not last year plus five percent. Demands issued correctly under the lease and the Landlord and Tenant Act, year-end accounts on time, and a reserve fund that is actually planned.

02

Repairs, contractors and cyclical works

Day-to-day repairs through a single reporting line. Cyclical decoration, roofs, lifts and plant planned ahead, specified properly and tendered under Section 20 where the law requires it.

Fire risk assessments and their actions, asbestos, legionella, lifts, electrical, and the Building Safety Act duties for higher-risk buildings, tracked, evidenced and reported to the accountable person.

04

Buildings insurance placed on the right basis, the rebuild value kept current, and claims handled from first notification to settlement.
