How a sale to us works.

Five steps. Most of the time is spent on the legal work, not on us.

1

Tell us about it

Address, what it is, how it is let, what is wrong with it and what you need from the sale. A phone call or a paragraph is enough to start.

2

Indicative figure in days

We look at the rent roll, the condition and the title, and give you a number and the assumptions behind it. If it is not going to work, we say so.

3

Visit and offer

One visit, then a written offer with the timetable you asked for. The figure does not move unless the building turns out to be different from what we were told.

4

Exchange

Our solicitors are instructed the day you accept. We exchange as fast as your side can, with a deposit paid.

5

Completion

On the date you need. Tenants are written to the same day and management starts immediately.
