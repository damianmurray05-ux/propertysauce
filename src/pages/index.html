Built for owners with more than a handful of doors.

Corporate and institutional landlords do not need an agent who is good with tenants. They need an operator whose reporting survives an audit, whose compliance survives an inspection and whose arrears process survives a tribunal.

Reporting

  • Monthly pack: rent roll, arrears ageing, voids, compliance status, works in progress
  • Client money held in a designated account, reconciled and reported
  • Data in your format, exported to your systems, not locked in ours

Compliance

  • Gas, electrical, EPC, fire risk, legionella, licensing: tracked per unit, renewed before expiry
  • Renters' Rights Act procedures built into every tenancy and every notice
  • Building Safety Act duties for higher-risk buildings mapped and evidenced

Income

  • Arrears acted on from the first missed payment, with an evidence file from day one
  • Voids turned in days, with pre-agreed refurbishment specifications and pricing
  • Rent reviews served correctly, once a year, against real comparables

Working with corporate and institutional landlords

Questions people ask before they instruct us.

Straight answers. If yours is not here, ring and ask.

Who do you work with?

Private landlords, corporate and institutional landlords, freeholders and owners of blocks and portfolios, and people selling residential property, particularly blocks of flats. Tenants of the homes we manage report repairs to us directly through the assistant on this site.

Do you buy blocks of flats?

Yes, for our own account, anywhere in England. Distressed, part-let, poorly managed or perfectly good stock; the difference is only the price. We can complete quickly where the seller needs certainty. How it works.

Do you take on block management?

Yes, for freeholders, right-to-manage companies and institutional owners: service charge budgets and accounts, contractors, health and safety, insurance and leaseholder communication. What is included.

Where do you operate?

Across England. The office is in Leyton, East London, and the properties we let, manage and own run from London and the Home Counties to the Midlands, Yorkshire and the North East.

How do tenants report a repair?

Through the assistant on this site. It checks the tenancy reference, sends a one-time code by email or text so we know who we are speaking to, records the problem with photographs, gives safety guidance for anything urgent and raises the job with the team. Emergencies should always be phoned in.
