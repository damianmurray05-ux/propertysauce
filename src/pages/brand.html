The Property Sauce brand.

Everything needed to reproduce the identity consistently: the mark, the wordmark, colour, type and voice, with files to download.

The mark.

A drop that reads as a roofline, with a paprika door. Property and sauce in one shape; a home with something warm inside it. It sits on a rounded tile so it holds up at favicon size and as a social avatar.

Primary, reversed

Rules

  • Clear space around the logo equal to the height of the tile. Minimum width 120px for the primary logo, 24px for the mark.
  • The mark is always the drop on a tile. Do not remove the tile, recolour the door, rotate the drop or add effects.
  • Use the reversed version on green or photographs. Never place the green logo on green.
  • The wordmark is set in Schibsted Grotesk Bold with tight tracking. Do not retype it in another font.

Colour.

Deep green for weight, paper for ground, paprika as the single accent. Two greys for text. Nothing else.

Type.

Schibsted Grotesk for headlines, tight and heavy. Geist for everything else. Both are open licence and self-hosted.

Schibsted Grotesk

Display. Bold (700) for headlines, tracking between -0.02em and -0.035em, line height around 1.

Geist

Body and interface. Regular (400) for copy, Semibold (600) for labels and buttons. Line height 1.6 for reading.

Voice.

Confident, dry, specific. Short sentences. Concrete detail over adjectives.

  • Say what we do and for whom. "We buy blocks of flats" beats "bespoke acquisition solutions".
  • Numbers only when they are real. No invented statistics, response times or percentages.
  • Never "nestled", "stunning", "luxurious", "boasts", "seamless", "elevate" or "unleash".
  • No em dashes. Use a full stop, a comma or a colon.
  • British spelling. Sentence case for headings. One exclamation mark per year, unused.
